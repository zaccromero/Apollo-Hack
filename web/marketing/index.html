A few things to know

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Does Apollo use real money?

No. Apollo is an educational game with simulated orders and a paper portfolio. It isn’t a brokerage account, and it doesn’t offer real-money trading or personalized investment advice.

Is the website example the actual app?

The blue Discover and comparison images are actual app captures. The guided website example is a separate, fictional teaching exercise. It doesn’t connect to your app account and resets when you reload.

How should I read the data?

Check each item’s source, date and limitations in the app. Illustrative demo prices and charts are separate from sourced research. Paper results are simulated and don’t predict real-world returns.
